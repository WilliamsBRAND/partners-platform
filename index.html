Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is it free to join?

Yes. You can create your partner account for free.

What can I promote?

You can promote any active course, programme, event, product or service shown inside your dashboard.

Can I promote more than one offer?

Yes. You can choose one offer or promote several offers from the same account.

How much can I earn?

Every offer has its own commission. The amount you will earn from each sale will be clearly shown before you start promoting it.

Do I need many followers?

No. You can start with your WhatsApp contacts, social media followers, communities or personal network.

How will my sales be tracked?

Every offer will have a unique link connected to your account. When someone buys through your link, the sale and commission will be recorded for you.

When can I withdraw my earnings?

You can request a withdrawal when your approved commission becomes available.

Where can I see my earnings?

Your dashboard will show your clicks, successful sales, pending commission, available balance and previous withdrawals.
